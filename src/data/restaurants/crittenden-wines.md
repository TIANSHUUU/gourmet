## en

Very nice food and vibes, enjoyed the view from the dining room looking out to the vineyards and lake. Simple modern Australian cuisine showcasing the quality of ingredients. Tried several wines, pretty decent, typical Mornington terroir.

Attentive service, staff looked a bit stressed and serious maybe bc it’s understaffed, but they always gave us a fast response, appreciated. A little pricey imo but a good choice for special occasions or celebrations.

## zh

食物和氛围都很棒，很喜欢从餐厅望出去的景色，能看到葡萄园和湖。简单的现代澳洲菜，突出了食材本身的品质。试了几款酒，相当不错，是典型的莫宁顿风土。

服务很周到，店员看起来有点压力大、神情严肃，可能是因为人手不足，不过他们总是很快地回应我们，很感谢。个人觉得有点贵，但对于特殊场合或庆祝来说是个不错的选择。
