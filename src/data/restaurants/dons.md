## en

Visited on a lovely Saturday afternoon for a pre-dinner bite and a drink. The wine selection here is great, the food's spot on, and the service is excellent — felt welcomed the second we walked in. Everything about today makes me want to come back for sure. Bonus: it's right by Prahran Market, so you can wander the stalls and grab a snack before heading here for a glass, or flip the order — either way it's a natural stop on a neighbourhood food crawl.

## zh

周六下午来的，赶在晚饭前先吃点小食喝杯酒。这里的酒单很赞，食物在线，服务一流，一进门就有种被热情招待的感觉，今天的体验让我毫不犹豫想要再来。而且离 Prahran Market 特别近，可以先逛逛市场、吃点小吃，再过来喝一杯，顺序倒过来也完全没问题，很适合安排进一趟社区觅食小旅程。
