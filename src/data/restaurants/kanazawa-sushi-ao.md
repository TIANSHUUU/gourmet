## en

Seems like it's a newly opened omakase in Kanazawa. Both the ingredient quality and the service were really great, good value for money, recommended! We had seven or eight pieces of sushi (the basic set we ordered didn't include nodoguro (blackthroat seaperch), so we added it), several dishes, and the dessert ice cream was really fun to watch being made. We ordered a local Kanazawa wine and it was unexpectedly really good. Hope we'll have the chance to come again in the future.

We booked on Ikyu — no need to call, which is really convenient for foreign travellers! Our Japanese is limited and the chef's English is limited, but we still finished the meal smoothly and happily. I'd suggest that anyone who doesn't speak Japanese first learn how some local ingredients are pronounced in Japanese. The chef is quite patient, and when there's a free moment you can chat a bit through a translation app haha.

## zh

在ikyu上预约了，不用打电话对外国旅客很方便！我们日语有限，chef英文有限，还是顺利愉快地吃完了这餐饭，建议不会日语的自己先了解一些当地食材的日文发音，厨师蛮有耐心的，有空的时候可以靠翻译软件交流一下哈哈。
