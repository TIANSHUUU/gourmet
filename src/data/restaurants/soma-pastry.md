## en

Aomori is apple country, so we worked our way through a lot of apple pies on this trip — the standard is high across the board, but this little shop's pie is easily one of the very best!! The place is full of Shōwa-era nostalgia, seemingly run single-handedly by the owner: a counter of affordable sweets by the entrance, and you can watch the pastries being made fresh in the back room. Takeaway only, no seating — and the owner couldn't have been kinder.

## zh

青森是苹果之乡，这趟我们一路吃了好多苹果派——整体水准都很高，但这家小店的绝对数一数二！！门店满满昭和怀旧感，似乎由店主一人打理：入口柜台摆着各式点心，价格实惠，还能看见甜品在后屋现做。只外带、无堂食，店主人也超级和善。
