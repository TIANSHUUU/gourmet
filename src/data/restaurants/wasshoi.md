## en

Popular lunch spot in the market, usually need to wait a bit for the table but the turnover is quick. Simple menu, you can choose rice or bread. The meats (chicken / beef / pork belly) have similar tsukune sauce flavor, which blends well with that final smoky touch. Tried 2 sakes of the month and really liked them. Friendly staff.
