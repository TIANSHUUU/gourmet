## en

Great ambiance and amazing food. We loved the open BBQ setting and the endless run of albums that soundtracked our whole night — straight from the chef's own collection! Bold flavours that had us plotting a return before we'd even left. The one slightly underwhelming note was the winter spritz cocktail — a touch too much ice, maybe.
