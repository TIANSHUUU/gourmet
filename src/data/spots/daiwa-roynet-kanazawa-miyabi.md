## en

Very close to the west exit of Kanazawa Station. The hotel already looks beautiful from the outside and in the lobby, with Kanazawa crafts and a classical beauty. The rooms are clean and fully equipped, and I especially loved that pair of craft-style cups. The shower and the toilet are separated, wet from dry, which is convenient.

## zh

距离金泽站西口很近。酒店从外观和大堂看起来就很漂亮，有金泽工艺品和古典美。房间干净、设施齐全，特别喜欢那对工艺品样式的杯子。淋浴间和厕所干湿分离，方便。
